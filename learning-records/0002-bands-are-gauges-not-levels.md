# Misconception surfaced: σ bands read as support/entry levels

After Lesson 5 the user asked whether price riding the bands makes them "a valid support or entry point", conflating statistical envelopes with cost-basis levels. Corrected: support means a cohort's breakeven (AVWAP, convergence, defended lows, levels with a *who* behind them); bands only grade extension, band-walking is a trend condition, and a band touch is never an entry. Buying +2σ is buying maximum extension at worst R.

**Implications:** Future lessons should keep contrasting "envelope vs. level" explicitly. When teaching the short side or selectivity, quiz on which lines have a defending cohort. Working rule taught: bands grade the move; cohort levels hold it; defense triggers it. Cross-link: [[0001-mission-is-swing-not-intraday]].
