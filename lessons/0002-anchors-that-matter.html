Lesson 0002 · Swing Trading with VWAP

Anchors That Matter

This lesson teaches one skill. Given a daily chart, choose an anchor point and justify it in one sentence. A defensible anchor makes the AVWAP mean something. An arbitrary one makes it decoration.

The principle

In Lesson 1 you read control off one line. But an AVWAP is only as meaningful as its starting point. It is the average cost basis of everyone who traded since the anchor, so the anchor must mark the moment a new cohort started building positions. StockCharts states the rule directly: anchor at events that "signal a change in market psychology, and the price action before that change should be excluded". The earlier action was driven by a different psychology, so averaging it in only pollutes the line.

That is also why volume matters. The AVWAP is volume-weighted, so an anchor at a heavy-volume repricing captures a large, real cohort of positions. Anchor at a quiet random Tuesday and the line averages almost nobody's decision. It will drift near price and hold no one's breakeven. Shannon's claim that the AVWAP is "the absolute truth of the relationship between a stock's supply and demand" is earned only when the anchor sits where supply and demand actually collided.

The shortlist

AnchorWhose cost basis it tracks
Earnings gap dayEveryone repricing the company on the new information. Huge volume, total repricing. The AVWAP from the gap says whether the market is accepting or rejecting the new valuation. The default swing anchor.
Breakout dayThe buyers who paid up for the breakout. Price holding above this line means they're right and defending. Losing it traps them.
Major swing high / lowEveryone positioned since the turn, the cohort riding the current leg. StockCharts' "significant turning point."
Big-news day, IPO daySame logic as earnings: a discrete event that forced a repricing on huge volume. For recent IPOs the IPO anchor is every public holder's average cost.
Year / quarter startNo psychology shift here, but institutions report performance by calendar, so year-to-date VWAP approximates the average entry of this year's flows. A convention worth having on the chart, not a thesis by itself.

Convergence: when lines agree

Run several anchors at once. When AVWAPs from different events converge at a price, that is "an especially strong area of support or resistance": two independent cohorts' breakevens stacked in one zone. TrendSpider's documentation of Shannon's indicator gives the same advice. Multiple anchors for a fuller view, price above the line treating it as support, below as resistance.

Keep the caveat An anchor justifies a line, not a trade. The Grimes caveat from Lesson 1 still applies. The AVWAP tells you where cohorts' breakevens sit and who controls the move. The entry itself needs structure and an invalidation, which is the next lesson.

Check yourself

A good anchor marks…

The anchor must mark the moment a new cohort started positioning. StockCharts: events that "signal a change in market psychology." Prior action belongs to a different psychology and is excluded.

The AVWAP anchored at an earnings gap tells you…

It is the volume-weighted average price paid by everyone repricing the stock since the report. Their collective breakeven, which is why price accepting or rejecting it matters.

When AVWAPs from two different anchors converge at a price…

Two independent cohorts share a breakeven there. StockCharts calls a convergence "an especially strong area of support or resistance."

Anchoring at a quiet, low-volume random day produces a weak line because…

The math works fine. But the line represents almost nobody's cost basis, so no cohort exists to defend it. Volume at and after the anchor is what gives the line teeth.

Do this before the next lesson

Extend the daily exercise. On one liquid stock, draw two AVWAPs, one from the latest earnings report and one from the most recent major swing low. Write two sentences: (1) the Lesson-1 control read on the earnings AVWAP; (2) where, if anywhere, the two lines converge and what zone that marks. Next lesson turns this into a trade: the pullback-to-AVWAP entry with an invalidation on daily closes.
