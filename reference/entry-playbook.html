Entry Playbook

Every entry shares one skeleton: control read + cohort level + evidence + pre-written invalidation. If any part is missing, it isn't a trade yet.

EntryPreconditionsTrigger (evidence)InvalidationNotes
Pullback
Lesson 3		 Buyers in control: price above a rising AVWAP from a defensible anchor; price returning toward the line Defense bar: probes the AVWAP zone, closes back up and away (often after multiple tests) Daily close below the AVWAP (or the defense bar's low) Best R of the family: buying the winning cohort's breakeven. SMB, Shannon
Reclaim
Lesson 6		 Price has been below the AVWAP; sellers' control is aging (higher lows pressing the line, shrinking distance below it) Cross plus hold: daily close above the line, then a higher low above it. The cross alone is noise Daily close back below the reclaimed line: the flip failed Catches turns earlier, fails more often. "Confirmation of the crossover and holding" per SMB; breakout-above-AVWAP framing in TrendSpider docs
Convergence pullback
Lessons 2 + 3		 As pullback, but the retreat is into a zone where two or more AVWAPs stack (e.g., earnings + swing-low anchors) Same defense bar, at the stacked zone Daily close below the lower line of the zone Stronger level: independent cohorts' breakevens stacked, "an especially strong area of support or resistance" per StockCharts
Breakout, AVWAP beneath
Lesson 6		 Price consolidating under resistance while holding well above a rising AVWAP: the cohort positioned to sustain a break Daily close through the resistance level (the AVWAP confirms; it does not trigger) Daily close back inside the base; trail later vs. the AVWAP per Lesson 4 Shannon: AVWAP helps "time breakouts and breakdowns" (Alphatrends archive). Worst-located stop of the family; size accordingly
Short mirrors
future lesson		 Sellers in control: price below a falling AVWAP Rejection at the line (failed rally into trapped longs' breakeven), or breakdown + failed reclaim Daily close back above the AVWAP Same skeleton inverted. Not yet taught, sketched only

Never in the family

  • Band touches. Envelopes have no defending cohort (LR 0002): bands grade the move; cohort levels hold it; defense triggers it.
  • First touches without defense. Location is not evidence.
  • Crosses without holds. The flip must prove itself.
  • Anything without a pre-written invalidation. The Grimes caveat stands: the claimed edge is the full skeleton, never the line itself.
