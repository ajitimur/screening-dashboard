Reference · Swing Trading with VWAP

Glossary

The course vocabulary. Every lesson uses these terms exactly as defined here.

TermDefinition
VWAP Volume-Weighted Average Price: the average price paid per share over a period, weighting each trade by its size. Cumulative Σ(price × volume) ÷ Σ(volume). The running "fair price" as actually transacted. StockCharts
Typical price (High + Low + Close) ÷ 3 for a bar. The per-bar price proxy charting platforms feed into the VWAP formula when trade-by-trade data isn't used.
Session VWAP VWAP computed from the regular-session open, resetting daily. When day traders say "VWAP" unqualified, they mean this. It is an intraday object, useless for multi-day holds, which is why this course anchors instead (see AVWAP).
Anchored VWAP (AVWAP) VWAP computed from a chosen event bar instead of the open: an earnings gap, a breakout, a swing high or low. The average price of everyone who has traded since that event. StockCharts, Shannon
Anchor The event bar an AVWAP starts from. Defensible anchors mark a shift in market psychology on heavy volume (earnings gaps, breakout days, major swing highs and lows, IPO day), so the line tracks a real cohort's cost basis. StockCharts
AVWAP convergence Two or more AVWAPs from different anchors meeting at a price. Independent cohorts' breakevens stacked in one zone, marking "an especially strong area of support or resistance." StockCharts
Execution benchmark The standard an institutional fill is graded against. VWAP is the dominant one: beat the session's VWAP and the desk "traded well." This grading is why large flows are scheduled around VWAP. Madhavan 2002
Volume curve The intraday U-shape of trading volume (heavy at open and close, light midday) that VWAP-tracking algos schedule their child orders against. Busseti & Boyd
In control (buyers) Price holding above a rising AVWAP: the average participant since the anchor is in profit and dips keep getting bought. The single-sentence read this course drills first.
In control (sellers) Price holding below a falling AVWAP: the average participant is underwater and rallies keep getting sold into.
Chop / no control Price crossing a flat AVWAP repeatedly. Neither side owns the move; control reads and pullback entries are off the table.
Reclaim Price losing the AVWAP, then decisively recovering above it and holding. A control flip, stronger evidence than the first cross.
Defense (of a level) Price probing an AVWAP zone and closing back up and away from it. Visible evidence the cohort anchored there is protecting its breakeven. On daily bars, the defense bar's low becomes the level they proved they'll fight for. SMB
R (risk unit) Entry price minus invalidation price, the amount lost if the thesis fails. Targets and results are measured in multiples of it. A setup paying less than ~2R to the obvious target isn't compensating the risk.
Relative strength vs. VWAP Comparing where a stock sits against its VWAP while the index sits against its own. A stock above VWAP while the market is below its VWAP is showing relative strength. SMB
Invalidation The pre-defined price condition that proves a trade's thesis wrong (e.g., "a daily close back below the earnings-anchored VWAP"). Defined before entry, always.
Trailing invalidation The exit level stepped up under each newly defended higher low as a winning move progresses. It only moves up, never down. A daily close below it ends the trade. Shannon, CMT podcast
Re-anchoring Planting a fresh AVWAP at the latest meaningful pivot as a move ages, to track the marginal cohort: the most recent buyers, first to defend or panic. The stale original line stays on as the disaster floor.
Extension Price stretched unusually far above (or below) its AVWAP, far from the crowd's cost basis. Late in a move it argues for scaling out into strength rather than initiating.
VWAP bands (σ bands) Standard-deviation envelopes around an AVWAP, from the volume-weighted variance since the anchor: σ² = Σ(v·p²)/Σv − VWAP². They bracket where the cohort actually transacted, turning "extended" into a statistical read (±1σ, ±2σ). Fat tails mean 2σ ≠ 95%, trends ride the bands, and bands widen as the anchor ages. Context, never a signal. TheVWAP guide
The Grimes caveat Shorthand in this course for: no free quantitative study shows a statistical edge in VWAP touches per se (Grimes 2015). VWAP is context and confirmation, not a standalone signal. SMB says the same.
