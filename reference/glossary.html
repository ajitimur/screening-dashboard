Reference · Swing Trading with VWAP

Glossary

The course vocabulary. Every lesson uses these terms exactly as defined here.

TermDefinition
VWAP Volume-Weighted Average Price: the average price paid per share over a period, weighting each trade by its size. Cumulative Σ(price × volume) ÷ Σ(volume). The session's running "fair price" as actually transacted. StockCharts
Typical price (High + Low + Close) ÷ 3 for a bar — the per-bar price proxy charting platforms feed into the VWAP formula when trade-by-trade data isn't used.
Session VWAP VWAP computed from the regular-session open, resetting daily. When day traders say "VWAP" unqualified, they mean this. It is an intraday object — useless for multi-day holds, which is why this course anchors instead (see AVWAP).
Anchored VWAP (AVWAP) VWAP computed from a chosen event bar instead of the open — an earnings gap, a breakout, a swing high/low. The average price of everyone who has traded since that event. StockCharts, Shannon
Anchor The event bar an AVWAP starts from. Defensible anchors mark a shift in market psychology on heavy volume — earnings gaps, breakout days, major swing highs/lows, IPO day — so the line tracks a real cohort's cost basis. StockCharts
AVWAP convergence Two or more AVWAPs from different anchors meeting at a price — independent cohorts' breakevens stacked in one zone, marking "an especially strong area of support or resistance." StockCharts
Execution benchmark The standard an institutional fill is graded against. VWAP is the dominant one: beat the session's VWAP and the desk "traded well." This grading is why large flows are scheduled around VWAP. Madhavan 2002
Volume curve The intraday U-shape of trading volume (heavy at open and close, light midday) that VWAP-tracking algos schedule their child orders against. Busseti & Boyd
In control (buyers) Price holding above a rising VWAP: the average participant since the anchor is in profit and dips keep getting bought. The single-sentence session read this course drills first.
In control (sellers) Price holding below a falling VWAP: the average participant is underwater and rallies keep getting sold into.
Chop / no control Price crossing a flat VWAP repeatedly. Neither side owns the session; control reads and pullback entries are off the table.
Reclaim Price losing VWAP, then decisively recovering above it and holding — a control flip, stronger evidence than the first cross.
Relative strength vs. VWAP Comparing where a stock sits against its VWAP while the index sits against its own — a stock above VWAP while the market is below its VWAP is showing relative strength. SMB
Invalidation The pre-defined price condition that proves a trade's thesis wrong (e.g., "a daily close back below the earnings-anchored VWAP"). Defined before entry, always.
The Grimes caveat Shorthand in this course for: no free quantitative study shows a statistical edge in VWAP touches per se (Grimes 2015). VWAP is context and confirmation, not a standalone signal — SMB says the same.
