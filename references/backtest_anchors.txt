The anchor check for the full run (PRD #182 Phase 6, issue #198)
================================================================

Re-run 2026-08-27, after #211 split the `in_field` anchor by the universe its
figure was counted over. The check now settles and writes its machine-readable
payload to `references/backtest_anchors.json` beside this file; earlier revisions
of this file recorded a refusal instead, which is why it was committed at all.

Run against `data/backtest.duckdb`, with the run's own field measurements in
`references/backtest_field_anchors.json`, and a written cause supplied for all
five anchors whose divergence has one:

    python -m backtest.anchors \

        --out-json references/backtest_anchors.json

The universe is not a flag. It comes off the field-measurements file, because
which of `in_field`'s two pins applies is a fact the run recorded about the gates
that screened its field — not a choice the person typing the command makes.

Five anchors diverge with a written cause. The sixth, `in_field` over the
contract's stateless universe, now **matches**: 165 of 503 against a committed
165 of 503, gap −5.00655 against a committed −5.01.

What changed is which pin the run is held to, and nothing else. No constant was
moved, no tolerance was widened, and the sign check was not waived. #211 measured
that `in_field` and findings §4b's gap are properties of the pair (rubric,
universe) rather than of the rubric — +1.95pp under the app's universe, −5.01pp
under the contract's — and that the reversal is the ADR20 floor and the trend
gate acting together, a property of the method under the contracted gates rather
than a defect in the field. Holding this run to §4b's app-universe figure was
therefore subtracting two numbers that were never the same number, and it
surfaced as a sign flip charged to the pipeline.

Read the stateless pin for what it is. It is a **first measurement**, made by the
run it now anchors, so it detects drift from here on rather than confirming this
run. What corroborates the run is on the other universe: §4b's own 397/656
(+1.95pp) reproduced exactly, and the same 503 names held fixed giving 324/503
(+1.86pp).
